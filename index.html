Faculty Block Guide

Abbreviations like LFTS, LFBA, and LFAMS represent major academic faculties at LPU. Learn where they are located.

LFTS

Lovely Faculty of Technology & Sciences

Primarily handles CSE, IT, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, Biotech, Food Tech, Pure Sciences, and Agriculture programs.

  • Location: Blocks 27-38 (Near Central Library/Gate 2)
  • Key Venues: Shanti Devi Mittal Auditorium, Uni-Mall auditoriums.
LFBA

Lovely Faculty of Business & Arts

Handles Business, Economics, Hotel Management, Fine Arts, Fashion Design, Law, Journalism, and Humanities.

  • Location: Blocks 14, 25, 26, & 30 (Middle Campus)
  • Key Venues: Baldev Raj Mittal Auditorium, Block 30 Seminar halls.
LFAMS

Lovely Faculty of Applied Medical Sciences

Handles Pharmaceutical Sciences, Ayurvedic Medicine, Physiotherapy, and Medical Lab Technology programs.

  • Location: Blocks 17, 18, & 19 (South Campus)
  • Key Venues: Block 18 Seminar Auditoriums.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining LPU as a fresher.

Yes, attendance at the LPU Induction Program is highly mandatory. During these days, you will be registered into the university systems, complete biometric verification, activate your LPU Touch portal, and get introduced to your faculty mentors and curriculum structure. Missing it will delay your integration into classes.

LPU has a strict zero-tolerance policy for ragging. You must submit a self-attested Anti-Ragging Affidavit signed by both you and your parent/guardian. You can fill out the form online on the anti-ragging web portal or LPUADMIT dashboard, print it, sign it, and bring the hard copy to campus during verification.

LPU provides a free pick-up service from Phagwara Railway Station, Jalandhar Cantt Railway Station, and Phagwara Bus Stand. You can book this service under 'Travel Arrangements' on your LPUADMIT portal. Ensure you book at least 48 hours prior to arrival so the transport team can coordinate your pickup.

Ensure that all outstanding fees are settled before physical reporting. Dues can be cleared online via the LPUADMIT portal. If you have queries about scholarship adjustments or loan updates, raise a query under the 'Ask Your Query' section on the portal or visit the accounts help desk at the reporting venue (Unipolis) immediately upon arrival.

While not mandatory for the first day of document verification, it is highly recommended to have a laptop for your coursework, especially for Engineering, Computer Applications, Management, and Design disciplines. You will need it to access study material, take exams, and complete UMS tasks.
