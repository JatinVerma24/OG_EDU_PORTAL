Step-by-Step Registration Guide

Follow these steps carefully on UMS to register. Tick them off to save your progress.

Step 1: Open UMS

Log into the LPU UMS Portal using your student registration credentials.

Step 2: Navigate to Examination System

Click on 'Academics' then go to the 'Examination System' sub-menu.

Step 3: Re-Appear Registration

Select 'Re-Appear & Improvement Registration', check your eligibility backlog subjects, and select your Mode.

Step 4: Payment Confirmation

Authorize payment. Double check if the fee amount was debited and status displays 'Successful'. Many students miss confirming this payment step!

Step 5: Download Confirmation Receipt

Always download the PDF transaction receipt for proof of registration in case of any future disputes.

Registration Modes Comparison

Select the mode that best matches your CA score and grade goals.

Mode A

₹500 / Subject

Without CA Improvement. Your existing UMS Continuous Assessment (CA) marks are carried forward.

  • Regular exam syllabus load
  • Great if you already scored 20+ in CA
  • Lower risk & lower registration cost

Mode B

₹3000 / Subject

With CA Improvement. Combines CA, MTE, and ETE into a single 70-mark comprehensive exam. Existing CA is ignored.

  • Complete syllabus coverage required
  • Best if CA score was extremely poor (< 15)
  • Enables maximum grade improvement possibility
