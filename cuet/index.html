CUET 2026 Score Range Guide

Expert strategies mapped for different CUET aggregate range buckets.

Low Range Admissions (150 - 300)

  • Focus on Delhi University Off-Campus Evening colleges and B.A. Programme language pairings.
  • Consider private or state government universities accepting CUET (e.g. Ambedkar University).
  • Add vocational degree courses (B.Voc) which feature lower cutoffs but practical placements.
  • Keep AMU / Harisingh Gour University on your priority registry.

Medium Range Admissions (300 - 500)

  • Secure admissions in South Campus DU colleges (e.g., Aurobindo, Motilal Nehru, Dyal Singh).
  • Choose less competitive Honors courses like Sanskrit, Philosophy, or Hindi Hons.
  • BHU B.A. Programme and B.Sc. Maths courses are high potential options in this range.
  • Fill maximum course combinations in Phase 2 to avoid allocation failure.

Good Range Admissions (500 - 650)

  • Excellent chance for South Campus top colleges (e.g. Sri Venkateswara, ARSD, DCAC).
  • North campus is accessible for B.Sc. physical science, life science, and language honors.
  • JNU B.A. Hons English and JMI B.Com Hons are competitive targets in this range.
  • Do not freeze early; continue to participate in subsequent CSAS rounds to upgrade.

North Campus vs South Campus DU

Key differentiators to help you decide your priority ranking orders.

North Campus DU

  • Colleges: SRCC, Hindu, Hansraj, Miranda House, Ramjas, Kirori Mal.
  • Cutoffs: Extremely high range (750+ to 790+ out of 800).
  • Atmosphere: Close-knit university enclave, heritage campus structures.
  • Placements: Leading corporate firms and finance consultancies.

South Campus DU

  • Colleges: Venky, LSR (in South Delhi), ARSD, Gargi, DCAC, Motilal.
  • Cutoffs: Moderate range (620+ to 730+ out of 800).
  • Atmosphere: Modern cityscape, scattered colleges, active cafe culture.
  • Placements: Growing rapidly, competitive placement cells.
