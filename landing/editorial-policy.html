Editorial Standards

Editorial Policy & Content Guidelines

Our commitment to transparency, factual verification, and responsible student-first education.

1. Mission & Educational Purpose

OGEDU AI is an independent, student-created educational platform built to reduce administrative confusion, provide transparent academic calculation models, and curate high-quality learning roadmaps for undergraduate engineering and business students.

Every tool, article, and roadmap published on this platform is designed to answer real student challenges—ranging from understanding official grade weight calculations to preparing for campus examinations and technical placement interviews.

2. Source Selection & Preference for Official Bodies

To ensure our academic guidance remains accurate and reliable, we adhere to strict sourcing criteria:

  • Official Academic Guidelines: Mathematical formulas for TGPA, CGPA, and exam criteria are strictly modeled after standard university ordinances, UGC guidelines, and AICTE curricular frameworks.
  • Standard Curricular Textbooks: Technical roadmaps (such as Data Structures, DBMS, Operating Systems, and Computer Networks) cite recognized academic standards (Cormen/Rivest, Silberschatz, Tanenbaum).
  • Public Examination Gazettes: Admission and scholarship advice draws directly from publicly released notifications from NTA, university admission cells, and official counseling brochures.

3. Curation vs. Verification Transparency

We believe in absolute honesty regarding third-party resources:

  • Curated Study Resources: In our YouTube Study Hub, channels and playlists are selected based on alignment with university syllabus modules. We describe them as curated educational resources rather than claiming blanket institutional verification.
  • External Link Distinctions: All external resources link directly to the creator's official YouTube channel or repository. We do not host, re-encode, or claim ownership of any third-party multimedia content.

4. Originality & Anti-Plagiarism Standards

All roadmaps, calculator documentation, and explanatory guides are written by student developers and contributors. We prohibit:

  • Copying articles or text verbatim from external websites or commercial blogs.
  • Mass-generating low-value automated text for search engine manipulation.
  • Presenting speculative estimations as guaranteed academic outcomes.

5. Calculator Limitations & Accuracy Commitments

Our academic calculators (TGPA, CGPA, Attendance, Exam Pass Checker) are tested against official grading scale matrices. However, university evaluation rules may introduce continuous assessment adjustments, relative grading scales, or departmental caveats.

Every tool page explicitly states its mathematical methodology, assumptions, and limitations, urging students to confirm official transcripts through their designated university portal.

6. Corrections & Feedback Procedure

Academic syllabi and grading regulations evolve. We actively review our resources each semester. If you spot an inaccurate credit weighting, an outdated playlist link, or a typo in any guide, we invite you to report it immediately.

To report a correction or suggest an improvement:

Email our editorial lead at jv242buisness@gmail.com with the subject line [Correction] [Page URL]. We review and update verified corrections within 24–48 hours.
