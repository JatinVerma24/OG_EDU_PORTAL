About Us

Simplifying Academic Navigation For Freshers and Seniors

Our Mission

OGEdu Portal (built by OG Jatin Verma) is a premium, open-source educational platform designed to solve the immediate informational gaps faced by college freshers and university students. Whether it's sorting through the complexities of admissions, mapping seat eligibilities, calculating cutoff percentiles, or tracking daily classroom attendance criteria — OGEdu compiles complex datasets into actionable tools.

Core Portals & Services

We provide multiple sub-modules built specifically for university students to guide them at every phase of their academic journey:

CUET Predictor

Calculate chances, generate CSAS preference sheets, and browse cutoffs.

FindOGlaptop

AI student advisor to search & compare specs based on course and budget.

Seniors Portal

Semester TGPA/CGPA calculations and LPU attendance projections.

Our Editorial & Verification Standards

Accuracy is our highest priority. All admission databases, seat matrices, relaxation rules, and cutoff tables are verified directly against official university publications (such as the Delhi University Common Seat Allocation System bulletin, NTA information brochures, and official university guidelines). Our tools are updated regularly to match dynamic regulatory shifts, ensuring you receive the most accurate data for decision-making.
