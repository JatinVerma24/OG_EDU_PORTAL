CUET 2026 Central University Counseling Strategy

OJ

OG Jatin Verma

Publisher & Senior Advisor • June 24, 2026

Securing a high percentile in CUET 2026 is only half the battle. The final seat allocation depends entirely on how strategically you lock your counseling forms. Here is our detailed counseling advice to maximize your chances of getting a premium seat at Delhi University (DU), BHU, or JNU.

DU CSAS Portal Phases

Delhi University processes allocations via its Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS) portal, divided into three crucial phases:

  • Phase I - Registration: Basic personal information and uploading CUET 2026 admit cards/scores. Ensure spellings match your class 12 certificates.
  • Phase II - Program & College Preferences: The most critical phase. Lock your preferred course-college combinations. You must list them from highest priority to lowest.
  • Phase III - Allocation & Admission: System checks your ranking and allocates the highest matching preference. You have a short window to accept.

Building Preference Lists

Avoid the common mistake of only listing top-tier colleges. Your list must have a balanced spread:

💡 Rule of Preference Listing: Place dream colleges at the top, realistic moderate cutoffs in the middle, and backup options at the bottom. Never rank a college higher just because its cutoff was lower last year.

Use the CUET Predictor tool on the OGEDU AI homepage to check previous years' raw cutoff ranks and see which programs match your category scores.

Freeze vs Upgrade Choice

When you are allocated a seat in Round 1, you must click "Accept". After verification by the college, you pay the fees. Once paid, you have two choices:

  • Freeze: You are fully satisfied with your seat. You lock it and exit subsequent allocation rounds.
  • Upgrade: You keep your allocated seat but opt to let the system re-evaluate you for higher preferences in next rounds. If upgraded, your previous seat is automatically surrendered. If not upgraded, you keep your Round 1 seat. (Highly recommended!).

Required Documents for Verification

Keep these digital scans and physical copies ready to prevent seat cancellations during college validation:

  • CUET 2026 Scorecard and admit card.
  • Class 10 & 12 passing certificates.
  • Category certificates (OBC-NCL, SC, ST, EWS) validated in the current financial year.
