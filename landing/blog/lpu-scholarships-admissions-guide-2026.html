LPU Scholarships & Induction reporting guide (2026)

OJ

OG Jatin Verma

Publisher & Senior Advisor • June 25, 2026

Welcome, Freshers of Batch 2026! Joining a large university campus can feel overwhelming. This guide is written to give you exact clarity on your admission reporting stages, document requirements, and scholarship structures so you don't get lost in administrative halls.

Reporting Roadmap

When you arrive on campus, you must follow a set sequence of allocations before you can enter your classes. Follow this 4-step reporting workflow:

  • Biometric Verification: Head directly to the Block 32/34 registration counters. Carry your physical provisional admission letter and registration slips.
  • Hostel Allocation: If you booked university housing, visit the allocation desks in the block assigned to your hostel (e.g. Block 15 for girls, Block 28 for boys) to collect your room keys.
  • Document Submission: Submit attested copies of school certificates and migration forms to the Registrar desk to finalize your enrollments.

Mandatory Document Checklist

Bypass delays by ensuring you have physical copies of these mandatory documents in your folder:

  • Provisional Admission Letter and fee receipts.
  • 10th & 12th Marks sheets (original + 3 photocopies).
  • Migration Certificate / Transfer Certificate.
  • Character Certificate from last school.
  • Aadhaar Card copy & 8 passport-size photographs.

Induction Week survival tips

Induction week is designed to help you settle in, but it can be exhausting. Here are key survival tips:

  • Use UMS Navigation: The university campus is massive. Download the UMS app and study classroom building codes.
  • Register early: Biometric queues get extremely long. Arrive at registration counters at 8:30 AM.
  • Join Peer Groups: Connect with seniors via the WhatsApp and Instagram groups listed on the OGEDU AI homepage to resolve quick daily doubts.
