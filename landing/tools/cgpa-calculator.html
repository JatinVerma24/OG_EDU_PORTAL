What is CGPA?

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is the metric used by universities in India and globally to measure an undergraduate or postgraduate student's cumulative academic performance across all completed semesters.

Unlike a simple arithmetic average of semester marks, CGPA is a credit-weighted mean. A 24-credit semester with intensive laboratory and theoretical components carries more weight in your overall CGPA than an 18-credit internship or elective semester.

Official Calculation Formula

Per university examination regulations (including UGC CBCS guidelines), CGPA is computed by multiplying the credits of each semester by the Term Grade Point Average (TGPA or SGPA) scored in that semester, summing those products, and dividing by the total credits attempted:

CGPA = Σ (Creditsi × TGPAi) / Σ Creditsi

Where:

  • Creditsi: Total registered academic credit units for semester i.
  • TGPAi (or SGPAi): Term Grade Point Average scored in semester i on a standard 10-point scale.
  • Σ Creditsi: Total cumulative credits attempted across all completed semesters.

Step-by-Step Realistic Example

Consider a B.Tech Computer Science student who has completed 3 semesters:

Semester Credits (C) TGPA (G) Credit Points (C × G)
Semester 1 22 7.80 171.60
Semester 2 24 8.50 204.00
Semester 3 20 8.20 164.00
Total 66 Credits — 539.60 Points

Calculation:

CGPA = 539.60 / 66 = 8.175 (Rounded to 8.18).

Important Academic Notes & Limitations

  • Audit & Non-Credit Courses: Environmental Studies (EVS), induction training, or zero-credit audit courses do not carry grade points and must not be counted in the credit total.
  • Backlogs & Re-appear Examinations: When you clear a reappear exam, your previous failing grade (F = 0) is replaced by the new grade, which recalculates both that semester's TGPA and your cumulative CGPA.
  • Conversion to Percentage: While AICTE guidelines specify Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10, some universities (including LPU) use Percentage = CGPA × 10. Always confirm with your university examination branch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SGPA, TGPA, and CGPA?
SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) and TGPA (Term Grade Point Average) refer to the grade point average achieved within a single semester or academic term. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) reflects performance across all completed semesters combined.
Does a 1-credit lab affect CGPA as much as a 4-credit theory subject?
No. A 4-credit course has four times the mathematical weight of a 1-credit laboratory course in determining your semester TGPA and final cumulative CGPA.
What CGPA is required for first-class degree with distinction?
Most technical universities grant First Class with Distinction for a CGPA of 8.50 or higher (provided all courses were cleared in the first attempt without backlogs), and First Class for a CGPA of 6.50 to 8.49.
