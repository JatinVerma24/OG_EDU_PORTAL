The Attendance Recovery Formula

If your current attendance is below your target percentage $T$ (typically 0.75 for 75%), every future class you attend increments both the numerator (present classes $P$) and the denominator (total classes $C$):

(Present + x) / (Total + x) ≥ Target Pct
For Target = 75%: x = 3 × Total − 4 × Present

Conversely, if your current attendance exceeds 75%, the maximum number of classes you can miss ($y$) while remaining at or above 75% is computed as:

Present / (Total + y) ≥ 0.75 ⇒ y = ⌊(4 × Present − 3 × Total) / 3⌋

Important University Regulations

  • Medical & Duty Leave: Approved Duty Leaves (for hackathons, cultural festivals, or sports representation) and medical leaves must be verified through your university student portal (such as LPU UMS or DU Samarth) to count toward condonation.
  • Detention & Debarment: Falling below 65% attendance typically results in debarment from the End-Term Examination, requiring a summer term or course re-registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the university condone attendance between 65% and 75%?
Most universities allow the Dean or Head of School to grant condonation up to 10% on valid medical grounds, certified hospitalisation, or official university duty representation.
Do laboratory sessions count separately from lectures?
Yes. In most technical curriculums, theory and laboratory attendance are tracked as independent components. A student must satisfy 75% in both the lecture course and the corresponding practical lab.
