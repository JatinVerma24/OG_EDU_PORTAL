Official University Passing Criteria

In credit-based undergraduate programs (such as B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Sc), passing a course requires satisfying two concurrent conditions:

Rule 1: External Examination Floor (30% Rule)

You must score at least 30% in the End-Term Exam (ETE) (or in some universities, a combined 30% across MTE + ETE). Scoring 100% in internal CA will NOT pass you if your end-term score is below 15 out of 50 marks.

Rule 2: Total Aggregate Threshold (40% Rule)

The sum total of your Continuous Assessment (CA), Mid-Term Exam (MTE), and End-Term Exam (ETE) must equal or exceed 40 marks out of 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my aggregate is 42% but I scored 12/50 in ETE?
You receive a Reappear / Backlog (Grade 'F'). Even though your total aggregate exceeds 40%, you failed the mandatory 30% floor requirement in the End-Term Examination (minimum 15 marks required).
Can good CA marks save me from failing?
High Continuous Assessment marks guarantee that once you hit the 30% floor in ETE, your total aggregate will comfortably clear 40% and secure a higher letter grade (B, A, or A+).
