Understanding TGPA & SGPA

Term Grade Point Average (TGPA), synonymous with Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA), is the weighted average of the grade points earned in all courses taken in a given term.

Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), each registered subject is allotted a specific number of academic credit units reflecting its weekly contact hours (e.g., 3 lectures + 1 tutorial = 4 credits; 2 lab hours = 1 credit).

TGPA = Σ (Creditsi × GradePointi) / Σ Creditsi

For each course, the grade points corresponding to your letter grade are multiplied by its credit value. The sum of these products across all semester courses is divided by total credits registered.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my TGPA if I get an 'F' grade in one subject?
An 'F' grade carries 0 grade points, but the credits of that course are still counted in the denominator (Σ Credits). This significantly lowers your TGPA. Once you clear the subject via a reappear exam, the new grade replaces the 0 and raises your TGPA.
How does TGPA convert to final CGPA?
Your final CGPA is the credit-weighted average of all semester TGPAs. Use our CGPA Calculator to calculate multi-semester cumulative averages.
