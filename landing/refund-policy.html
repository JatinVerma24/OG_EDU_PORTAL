Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. Free Educational Services

OGEdu Portal is an open-access academic resource hub. The primary tools, including the Freshers pre-induction checklist, Seniors TGPA/CGPA result calculator, FindOGlaptop specs comparison tool, and CUET college eligibility predictor, are offered completely free of charge. No payment details are ever requested to use these core features.

2. Optional Paid Counseling & Digital Goods

From time to time, OGEdu Portal may offer premium individual counseling sessions, webinars, or downloadable admission guidebooks. In such cases, the following terms apply:

  • Digital Guidebooks & PDFs: Due to the digital nature of downloadable guidebooks, all sales are final. Once a download link is provided or emailed, we cannot issue a refund.
  • Counselling Sessions: If you schedule a paid one-on-one session, you may cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled start time. Cancellations requested less than 24 hours before the session are not eligible for a refund.

3. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To cancel a scheduled counseling appointment or report a billing dispute, please reach out to us at jv242buisness@gmail.com with your name, registered email, transaction date, and booking reference. We will evaluate refund requests within 5-7 business days.
