Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our calculators, student data privacy, and tools.

How does the Student ID classification work?
Our client-side algorithm inspects the prefix of the Student Registration ID you type (e.g. 126, 261 for Freshers, 123/124/125 for Seniors) directly in your browser. No registration or student data is transmitted or stored on any server.
Are the GPA & Pass criteria calculators official?
Yes, the calculator formulas are mapped strictly according to university academic regulations: ETE ≥ 30% (or combined MTE+ETE ≥ 30%) with an overall score ≥ 40%. The calculations mirror the official evaluation system accurately.
Do I need to sign up or create an account?
No! We completely removed Firebase authentication and sign-in walls from the entire platform. Every portal (OGTOOLS, Notes Vault, Laptop Finder) is 100% open and immediately accessible.
