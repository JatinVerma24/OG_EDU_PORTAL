Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

OGEdu Portal uses cookies for several reasons, including preserving your preferences and analyzing traffic. The categories of cookies used are:

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are critical to make our site operate. For example, we use local storage to securely save your student batch preferences or ID layout classification so you do not have to retype them on every visit.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: We use Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our calculators and guides. This helps us optimize performance and page speeds.
  • Advertising & Marketing Cookies: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. These ads are tailored to be more relevant and prevent showing duplicate ads.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Third-party services include:

  • Google AdSense: Serves personalized and non-personalized advertisements. You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Google Analytics: Collects aggregated anonymous traffic statistics. You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at Google Opt-out page.

4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. Disabling cookies may prevent you from taking full advantage of some interactive features on OGEdu Portal.

To find out more about how to manage cookies on popular browsers, you can visit the respective developer documentation (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
