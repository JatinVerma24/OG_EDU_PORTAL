How to Prepare with These Resources

Exam preparation playbook tested across hundreds of university seniors

1. Master Units 1 & 2 First

In MTH165, PHY175, and CHE110, Units 1 and 2 make up over 70% of the midterm exam. Do not touch Unit 3 until your foundation on the first two units is rock solid.

2. Solve Past Year Papers (PYQs)

We have attached 11 scanned PYQ papers for CHE110 and MEC136. Over 40-50% of the drawing and numerical questions repeat verbatim with changed values.

3. Python 220 Question Bank

For INT108 Python, practice the 220 Question Bank included above. It covers tricky slicing, mutable vs immutable traps, and output prediction that teachers put in question papers.
