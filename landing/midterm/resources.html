The 80/20 Mid-Term Preparation Playbook

How senior toppers secure 20+/30 marks with minimal effort

Step 1: Finish Units 1 & 2 First

In almost all university midterm papers (MTH165, PHY175, CHE110), Units 1 and 2 comprise 70% to 75% of the question paper. Don't waste time on Unit 3 until Units 1 & 2 are 100% prepared.

Step 2: Solve the 11 PYQs

We have attached 11 scanned PYQ papers for CHE110, MEC136, and PHY175. Over 40% of the derivations and numericals repeat with identical questions or changed variable values.

Step 3: INT108 220 Qs Goldmine

Teachers love asking tricky output questions on Python string slicing and mutable tuple-lists. Solve the 220 Question Bank included above to avoid unexpected deductions.
