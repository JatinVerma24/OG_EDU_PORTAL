Important Instructions for Online Class (5, 6 & 7 October)
Auto-Attendance Logging

Aapko class ke beech me "Present Sir / Present Ma'am" bolne ki zaroorat nahi hai. LPU My Class platform aapke logged-in active duration ko automatically track karke UMS attendance me mark karta hai.

Camera On / Off Rule

Camera on karna ya na karna purely teacher ke instruction par depend karta hai. Agar faculty prompt kare camera on karne ko, toh camera zaroor on karein, warna backend verification issue ho sakta hai!

Class Day Preparation & Best Practices Checklist

1 / 8 Completed
Step 1Prerequisite

Verify Updated Timetable on UMS & LPUTouch

Log in to UMS (ums.lpu.in) or open the LPUTouch app. Navigate to "My Timetable" and check "UMS My Messages" for the updated schedule specifically active for 5, 6, and 7 October 2026.

Pro-Tip: Confirm course codes, slot timings (e.g. 09:00 AM - 10:00 AM), and designated faculty names beforehand.
Step 2Access Portal

Access the LPU My Class Platform

Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on your laptop/desktop (recommended) and navigate to myclass.lpu.in, or tap the "My Class" quick link in UMS / LPUTouch app.

Pro-Tip: Login with your University Registration Number and UMS Password. If required, authenticate via your university Google/Outlook ID.
Step 3Hardware Check

Perform Pre-Flight Audio & Network Check

Ensure your internet connection is stable (minimum 5-10 Mbps). Grant camera and microphone permissions when prompted by your browser.

Pro-Tip: Keep earphones/headphones ready to eliminate mic feedback and background echo. Keep a mobile hotspot hotspot as backup.
Step 4Punctuality

Join Class 5 Minutes Prior to Scheduled Slot

Under your My Class dashboard, locate your subject card with the active "Live Now" or "Join" button. Click to enter the virtual lecture room 5 minutes early.

Pro-Tip: Enter with your microphone MUTED. Enable video only if explicitly requested by your course instructor.
Step 5100% Mandatory

Compulsory Attendance Compliance (CRITICAL)

Attendance in online classes on 5, 6, and 7 October 2026 is strictly COMPULSORY. The platform logs your active connection duration automatically.

Pro-Tip: Stay connected for the entire duration. Do not exit early. Respond immediately to roll calls or in-class attendance verification polls.
Step 6Engagement

Use Interactive Features & Raise Hand for Doubts

Use the "Raise Hand" icon to request permission to speak. Post academic questions in the live chat box and download slides from the shared files repository.

Pro-Tip: Keep chat clean and academic. Faculty records chat logs for participation points.
Step 7Self Study

Access Recorded Lectures for Post-Class Revision

If you experienced brief power cuts or bandwidth drops, check the "Recorded Lectures" tab on LPU My Class. Recordings are usually uploaded within 2 to 4 hours.

Pro-Tip: Use recordings to clarify formulas and concepts before the doubt sessions on 8 & 9 October.
Step 8Resolution

Raise RMS Ticket for Any Attendance or Tech Issue

If your session crashed or your attendance failed to reflect on UMS within 24 hours, file an RMS ticket under Category: Academics, Sub Category: Online Classes.

Pro-Tip: Take a screenshot of your connection error or timestamp and attach it to the RMS ticket.
