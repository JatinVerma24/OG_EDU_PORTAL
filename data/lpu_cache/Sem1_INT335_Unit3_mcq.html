1 What is the main purpose of divergent thinking?

Divergent Thinking and Idea Generation Easy
A. To generate many possible ideas
B. To select one final idea
C. To test a finished product
D. To prepare a project budget

Correct Answer: To generate many possible ideas

Explanation:

Divergent thinking explores a wide range of possible ideas or solutions.

Incorrect! Try again.

2 Which activity best demonstrates divergent thinking?

Divergent Thinking and Idea Generation Easy
A. Ranking ideas by their feasibility
B. Testing a single working prototype
C. Listing many uses for a paper clip
D. Choosing the cheapest proposed design

Correct Answer: Listing many uses for a paper clip

Explanation:

Thinking of many different uses encourages variety and creative exploration.

Incorrect! Try again.

3 What does convergent thinking mainly involve?

Convergent and Divergent Thinking Easy
A. Avoiding comparison between alternatives
B. Combining unrelated words at random
C. Selecting the most suitable solution
D. Producing as many ideas as possible

Correct Answer: Selecting the most suitable solution

Explanation:

Convergent thinking narrows multiple possibilities to the most suitable solution.

Incorrect! Try again.

4 Which sequence is commonly used during creative problem solving?

Correct Answer: Diverge first, then converge

Explanation:

Teams usually generate many ideas first and then narrow them down.

Delaying criticism helps participants contribute ideas freely during brainstorming.

6 What is a basic goal of brainstorming?
