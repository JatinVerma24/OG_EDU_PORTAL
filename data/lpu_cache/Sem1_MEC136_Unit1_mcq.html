Unit 1: Introduction to Engineering Drawing - Practice Quiz

MEC136 — Engineering Drawing With Autocad 50 Questions

1 Which grade of pencil is typically used for drawing center lines and section lines to ensure they are thin and sharp?

A. 2H or 3H
B. HB
C. 6B
D. B

Correct Answer: 2H or 3H

Explanation:

Harder pencils like 2H or 3H are used for drawing thin, sharp, and light lines such as center lines, dimension lines, and construction lines.

Incorrect! Try again.

2 The T-square is primarily used to draw which type of lines?

A. Vertical lines
B. Inclined lines
C. Horizontal lines
D. Curved lines

Correct Answer: Horizontal lines

Explanation:

The T-square slides along the edge of the drawing board and is used to draw horizontal parallel lines.

Incorrect! Try again.

3 Which instrument is used to transfer dimensions from a scale to the drawing paper with high precision?

A. Clinograph
B. Protractor
C. Divider
D. Compass

Correct Answer: Divider

Explanation:

A divider has two needle points and is used specifically for transferring distances or dimensions from a scale to a drawing.

Incorrect! Try again.

4 In engineering drawing, visible outlines or object lines are represented by which type of line?

A. Dashed thin line
B. Continuous thick line
C. Continuous thin line
D. Long dashed dotted line

Correct Answer: Continuous thick line

Explanation:

Visible edges and outlines of an object are drawn using continuous thick lines (Type A lines) to make them stand out.

Incorrect! Try again.

5 A line composed of a long dash followed by a dot and then a long dash represents:

A. Dimension lines
B. Center lines
C. Hidden edges
D. Cutting planes

Correct Answer: Center lines

Explanation:

Center lines (or lines of symmetry) are represented by a chain thin line (long dash, dot, long dash).

Incorrect! Try again.

6 Hidden edges of an object are drawn using:

A. Wavy lines
B. Short dashed lines
C. Continuous thick lines
D. Long dashed dotted lines

Correct Answer: Short dashed lines

Explanation:

Hidden lines, representing edges not directly visible from the viewing angle, are drawn as dashed lines (Type E or F).

Incorrect! Try again.

7 Which line type is used to indicate a cutting plane?

A. Continuous thick line
B. Chain thin line with thick ends
C. Continuous thin wavy line
D. Dashed line

Correct Answer: Chain thin line with thick ends

Explanation:

Cutting planes are shown by a chain thin line that is thickened at the ends and at changes of direction.

Incorrect! Try again.

8 The standard length-to-width ratio of an arrowhead in dimensioning is:

A. 3:1
B. 4:1
C. 2:1
D. 1:1

Correct Answer: 3:1

Explanation:

In engineering drawing standards, the length of the arrowhead should be roughly three times its width.

Incorrect! Try again.

9 In the 'Aligned System' of dimensioning, how are the dimensions placed?

A. Perpendicular to the dimension line
B. Horizontal only, readable from the bottom
C. Inside the object boundaries only
D. Parallel to the dimension line and readable from the bottom or right

Correct Answer: Parallel to the dimension line and readable from the bottom or right

Explanation:

In the Aligned System, dimensions are placed parallel to the dimension line so they can be read from the bottom or the right-hand side of the drawing sheet.

Incorrect! Try again.

10 The symbol 'Ø' in dimensioning indicates:

A. Diameter
B. Depth
C. Square
D. Radius

Correct Answer: Diameter

Explanation:

The symbol Ø (Phi) is universally used to denote the diameter of a circle or hole.

Incorrect! Try again.

11 Extension lines should extend how far beyond the dimension line?

A. Approx. 100 mm
B. They should stop exactly at the dimension line
C. 3 mm to 5 mm
D. 10 mm

Correct Answer: 3 mm to 5 mm

Explanation:

Extension lines should extend slightly past the dimension line, typically about 3 mm, to clearly define the range of the dimension.

Incorrect! Try again.

12 What does 'Single Stroke' mean in lettering?

A. Only one font is used
B. The thickness of the line equals the width of the pen tip
C. Letters are written in one continuous horizontal line
D. The letter is written without lifting the pen

Correct Answer: The thickness of the line equals the width of the pen tip

Explanation:

Single stroke means the thickness of the line of the letter is obtained in one stroke of the pencil or pen, resulting in uniform thickness.

Incorrect! Try again.

13 Vertical Gothic lettering implies that the stems of the letters are:

A. Thick and Thin (Serif)
B. Italicized
C. Perpendicular to the horizontal guidlines
D. Inclined at 75 degrees

Correct Answer: Perpendicular to the horizontal guidlines

Explanation:

Vertical lettering means the upright strokes are perpendicular (90 degrees) to the horizontal baselines.

Incorrect! Try again.

14 Representative Fraction (RF) is defined as:

A. Area of Drawing / Actual Area
B. Actual Length / Drawing Length
C. Drawing Length / Actual Length
D. Actual Length x Drawing Length

Correct Answer: Drawing Length / Actual Length

Explanation:

RF is the ratio of the length of the object on the drawing to the actual length of the object, with both units being the same.

Incorrect! Try again.

15 A scale of 1:50 is an example of:

A. Reducing Scale
B. Enlarging Scale
C. Diagonal Scale
D. Full Scale

Correct Answer: Reducing Scale

Explanation:

1:50 means 1 unit on the drawing represents 50 units in reality, which reduces the size of the representation.

Incorrect! Try again.

16 Which scale is used to represent three consecutive units (e.g., meters, decimeters, centimeters)?

A. Vernier Scale
B. Plain Scale
C. Chord Scale
D. Diagonal Scale

Correct Answer: Diagonal Scale

Explanation:

A diagonal scale is used to measure very small subdivisions and can read up to three consecutive units (or to two decimal places).

Incorrect! Try again.

17 A Plain Scale is capable of reading how many units?

A. One unit only
B. Angles
C. Three units
D. Two units or a unit and its subdivision

Correct Answer: Two units or a unit and its subdivision

Explanation:

Plain scales are constructed to measure two units (e.g., cm and mm) or a unit and its first subdivision.

Incorrect! Try again.

18 The principle used in the construction of a Diagonal Scale is:

A. Pythagoras theorem
B. Golden ratio
C. Principle of similar triangles
D. Archimedes principle

Correct Answer: Principle of similar triangles

Explanation:

Diagonal scales rely on the principle of similar triangles to divide a short line segment into equal parts.

Incorrect! Try again.

19 If an object 10m long is represented by a line 10cm long, the R.F. is:

A. 10:1
B. 1:1000
C. 1:10
D. 1:100

Correct Answer: 1:100

Explanation:

10cm / 10m = 10cm / 1000cm = 1/100.

Incorrect! Try again.

20 The default file extension for an AutoCAD drawing file is:

A. .dwg
B. .dwt
C. .dxf
D. .pdf

Correct Answer: .dwg

Explanation:

.dwg is the native file format for AutoCAD drawing data.

Incorrect! Try again.

21 Which key is pressed to toggle 'Ortho Mode' on and off in AutoCAD?

A. F7
B. F3
C. F8
D. F10

Correct Answer: F8

Explanation:

F8 is the function key assigned to toggle Ortho mode, which restricts cursor movement to vertical and horizontal directions.

Incorrect! Try again.

22 The command used to set the drawing units in AutoCAD is:

A. SET
B. DIM
C. SCALE
D. UNITS

Correct Answer: UNITS

Explanation:

Type 'UNITS' (or 'UN') to open the Drawing Units dialog box to define precision and measurement type.

Incorrect! Try again.

23 What is the purpose of the 'LIMITS' command in AutoCAD?

A. To restrict the zoom level
B. To limit file size
C. To limit the number of layers
D. To set the boundary of the drawing area

Correct Answer: To set the boundary of the drawing area

Explanation:

LIMITS sets the lower-left and upper-right corners of the drawing area (grid display area).

Incorrect! Try again.

24 In AutoCAD, the Cartesian coordinate system uses which axes for 2D drafting?

A. X and Y
B. X, Y, and Z
C. X and Z
D. Y and Z

Correct Answer: X and Y

Explanation:

2D drafting primarily utilizes the X (horizontal) and Y (vertical) axes.

Incorrect! Try again.

25 To specify a Relative Polar Coordinate in AutoCAD, the syntax used is:

A. @x,y
B. x,y
C. #distance,angle
D. @distance<angle

Correct Answer: @distance<angle

Explanation:

The '@' symbol indicates relative to the last point, and '<' introduces the angle (e.g., @50<45).

Incorrect! Try again.

26 What does UCS stand for in AutoCAD?

A. User Control Settings
B. User Coordinate System
C. Universal Computer System
D. Unified CAD Standard

Correct Answer: User Coordinate System

Explanation:

UCS stands for User Coordinate System, which defines the origin and orientation of the X, Y, and Z axes in the drawing space.

Incorrect! Try again.

27 Which function key toggles the Object Snap (OSNAP) mode?

A. F5
B. F9
C. F1
D. F3

Correct Answer: F3

Explanation:

F3 turns running Object Snaps on and off.

Incorrect! Try again.

28 The geometric shape displayed by OSNAP when snapping to a Midpoint is a:

A. Square
B. Circle
C. Diamond
D. Triangle

Correct Answer: Triangle

Explanation:

In AutoCAD, a triangle indicates the Midpoint object snap.

Incorrect! Try again.

29 The geometric shape displayed by OSNAP when snapping to an Endpoint is a:

A. Circle
B. Triangle
C. Cross
D. Square

Correct Answer: Square

Explanation:

In AutoCAD, a square indicates the Endpoint object snap.

Incorrect! Try again.

30 Which AutoCAD command allows you to move the drawing view without changing the coordinates or magnification?

A. ZOOM
B. PAN
C. ROTATE
D. MOVE

Correct Answer: PAN

Explanation:

PAN (often accessed by holding the middle mouse button) shifts the view of the drawing laterally.

Incorrect! Try again.

31 The F7 key in AutoCAD is used to:

A. Toggle Grid Display
B. Toggle Ortho
C. Toggle Help
D. Toggle Snap

Correct Answer: Toggle Grid Display

Explanation:

F7 turns the background grid on and off.

Incorrect! Try again.

32 Which coordinate entry method is represented by entering '10,20' directly in the command line?

A. Relative Rectangular
B. Absolute Rectangular
C. Relative Polar
D. Direct Distance

Correct Answer: Absolute Rectangular

Explanation:

Entering simple x,y coordinates relates directly to the origin (0,0), making it Absolute Rectangular coordinates.

Incorrect! Try again.

33 What is the function of the 'Zoom All' command?

A. Zooms to fit the drawing limits or extents
B. Zooms to a specific window
C. Zooms in by 2x
D. Zooms to the last view

Correct Answer: Zooms to fit the drawing limits or extents

Explanation:

Zoom All (Z > A) adjusts the magnification of the drawing area to show the entire drawing limits or the drawing extents, whichever is larger.

Incorrect! Try again.

34 Which F-key is used to access the AutoCAD Help menu?

A. F12
B. F1
C. F11
D. F2

Correct Answer: F1

Explanation:

F1 is the standard Windows convention for Help, used in AutoCAD as well.

Incorrect! Try again.

35 What does the '@' symbol mean when entering coordinates in AutoCAD?

A. Absolute coordinate
B. Relative to the origin (0,0)
C. Polar angle
D. Relative to the last point entered

Correct Answer: Relative to the last point entered

Explanation:

The '@' symbol tells AutoCAD to calculate the new point relative to the last point placed.

Incorrect! Try again.

36 Which OSNAP setting would you use to snap to the quadrant points (0, 90, 180, 270 degrees) of a circle?

A. Center
B. Tangent
C. Perpendicular
D. Quadrant

Correct Answer: Quadrant

Explanation:

The Quadrant snap targets the 0, 90, 180, and 270-degree points on a circle or arc.

Incorrect! Try again.

37 The command line window in AutoCAD can be hidden or shown by pressing:

A. Ctrl + 9
B. Ctrl + P
C. Ctrl + S
D. Ctrl + C

Correct Answer: Ctrl + 9

Explanation:

Ctrl + 9 is the shortcut toggle for the visibility of the command line palette.

Incorrect! Try again.

38 F9 key is used for:

A. Grid
B. Ortho
C. Polar
D. Snap Mode

Correct Answer: Snap Mode

Explanation:

F9 toggles Snap Mode, which constrains the cursor to move in specific intervals (usually tied to the grid).

Incorrect! Try again.

39 Which direction are angles measured in AutoCAD by default?

A. Horizontal
B. Counter-Clockwise
C. Clockwise
D. Vertical

Correct Answer: Counter-Clockwise

Explanation:

By default, AutoCAD measures positive angles in a counter-clockwise direction starting from the positive X-axis (East).

Incorrect! Try again.

40 Which AutoCAD interface element displays the cursor coordinates and drafting aid buttons (Grid, Ortho, Osnap)?

A. The Ribbon
B. The Quick Access Toolbar
C. The Status Bar
D. The Command Line

Correct Answer: The Status Bar

Explanation:

The Status Bar is located at the bottom of the application window and contains coordinates and toggles for drafting settings.

Incorrect! Try again.

41 A 2:1 scale indicates that the drawing is:

A. Half the size of the object
B. The same size as the object
C. Twice the size of the object
D. One tenth the size

Correct Answer: Twice the size of the object

Explanation:

2 units on drawing = 1 unit in reality. This is an enlarging scale.

Incorrect! Try again.

42 The lower-left corner of the drawing limits is usually set to:

A. Negative values
B. 0,0
C. 10,10
D. 100,100

Correct Answer: 0,0

Explanation:

Standard practice is to set the lower-left corner at the origin (0,0).

Incorrect! Try again.

43 Which key toggles Polar Tracking?

A. F10
B. F11
C. F12
D. F8

Correct Answer: F10

Explanation:

F10 toggles Polar Tracking, which restricts cursor movement to specified angles.

Incorrect! Try again.

44 In lettering, the height of lower-case letters relative to capital letters (approximate ratio) is often:

A. 2/3 to 3/4 of capital height
B. 1/2 of capital height
C. 1/4 of capital height
D. Same height

Correct Answer: 2/3 to 3/4 of capital height

Explanation:

While standards vary, lower case stems generally reach the capital height, but the body is typically about 2/3 to 3/4 (or 7/10 per IS standards) of the height of capital letters.

Incorrect! Try again.

45 A continuous thin line drawn freehand is used to represent:

A. Center line
B. Dimension line
C. Visible edges
D. Limits of partial or interrupted views (Break line)

Correct Answer: Limits of partial or interrupted views (Break line)

Explanation:

A freehand continuous thin line is used as a break line to show short breaks or irregular boundaries.

Incorrect! Try again.

46 What is the function of the 'Escape' (Esc) key in AutoCAD?

A. Cancel current command
B. Open help
C. Repeat last command
D. Save file

Correct Answer: Cancel current command

Explanation:

Esc is used to cancel the active command or deselect objects.

Incorrect! Try again.

47 The WCS icon typically appears in which corner of the drawing area?

A. Bottom-Left
B. Top-Left
C. Bottom-Right
D. Top-Right

Correct Answer: Bottom-Left

Explanation:

The UCS/WCS icon is anchored in the bottom-left corner of the viewport by default.

Incorrect! Try again.

48 If you need to draw a line exactly 45 degrees from the horizontal, but Ortho is ON, what happens?

A. The line becomes curved
B. You can draw the line easily
C. AutoCAD crashes
D. The line is forced to 0 or 90 degrees

Correct Answer: The line is forced to 0 or 90 degrees

Explanation:

Ortho mode strictly enforces horizontal (0/180) or vertical (90/270) movement; it prevents diagonal drawing with the mouse unless coordinate overrides are used.

Incorrect! Try again.

49 Which instrument is NOT typically used for linear measurement?

A. Protractor
B. Scale (Ruler)
C. Vernier Scale
D. Diagonal Scale

Correct Answer: Protractor

Explanation:

A protractor is used for measuring angles, not linear distances.

Incorrect! Try again.

50 In a diagonal scale, the vertical division of the short end is divided into how many parts to measure the second decimal (if measuring meters to cm)?

A. 10
B. 50
C. 5
D. 100

Correct Answer: 10

Explanation:

To achieve the next level of precision (e.g., 1/100th of the main unit), the vertical height is divided into 10 equal parts.

Incorrect! Try again.
