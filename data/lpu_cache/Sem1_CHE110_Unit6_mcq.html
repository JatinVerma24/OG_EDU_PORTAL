1 What does human population growth mean?

Human population growth Easy
A. Increase in the number of people over time
B. Increase in the average height of people
C. Change in the age of one person
D. Movement of people between two cities

Correct Answer: Increase in the number of people over time

Explanation:

Human population growth is the increase in the total number of people in a population over a period of time.
