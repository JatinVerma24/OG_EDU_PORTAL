Course Overview

INT335 2 Credits L:2 T:0 P:0 Elective Pool 2
Download Syllabus

This subject introduces human-centered design thinking, including user research, empathy, problem definition, ideation, prototyping, validation, customer journey analysis, and iterative product redesign. Students apply design thinking tools to develop, evaluate, and present solutions to real-world problems.

Course Description

This subject introduces human-centered design thinking, including user research, empathy, problem definition, ideation, prototyping, validation, customer journey analysis, and iterative product redesign. Students apply design thinking tools to develop, evaluate, and present solutions to real-world problems.
