1 What does learning generally involve?

Learning and Creative Thinking Easy

B. Keeping existing behavior unchanged
C. Repeating facts without understanding
D. Avoiding all new experiences

Explanation:

Learning is the process of gaining knowledge, skills, attitudes, or understanding through study and experience.

Creative thinking involves exploring different ideas and generating multiple possible solutions.

Brain Processing Dynamics Easy

C. Market validation
D. Functional fixedness

Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to form and reorganize neural connections through learning and experience.

9 What is functional fixedness?

Explanation:

Changing perspective can reveal new possibilities and help a person move beyond established assumptions.
